CUt with delimiters:

cut -d ":" -f 2 file.txt

Cut without delimiters (to display n characters of bytes of a line):
cut -c 10 file.txt

Cut and sort(remember sort works only on files not on patterns entered in the command): 
cut -d ":" -f 4 employees.txt >> employee_salaries.txt && sort -n employee_salaries.txt
