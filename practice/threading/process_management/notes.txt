fork() is fairly simple, so no discussion. 
exec() has a few different types, but they all revolve around 4 basic options:
l: specify a list of arguments one by one 
execl("program_path", arg1, arg2..... NULL);
execl("/bin/ls", "ls", "-l", NULL);
v: specify a vector of arguments
char *arg[] = {arg0, arg1, arg2}
execv("program_path", arg);
char *ls_args[] = {"ls", "-l", NULL};
execv("/bin/ls",ls_args);	
p: Search PATH. No need to specify the path to the file.
execlp("ls","ls", "-l", NULL);
